# Unit 19 · Animals 動物

豬 馬 羊 雀仔 老虎 熊貓 馬騮 (猴子 in writing) 兔仔 蛇 老鼠 龍, with unit 1's 牛 魚 雞 and unit 5's 貓 狗; 動物園; the twelve zodiac animals, 生肖 and 屬. Grammar point: 隻 vs 條 for animals; 會 + verb (雀仔會飛, 魚會游水), 會唔會, 邊隻.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u19-learn`).
- `zoo.html`, `zoo.js` (`u19-zoo`): Zoo: hear an animal and find it, say one with 隻 or 條 (`Measures.round`), answer 會唔會 about an animal, find the one that can fly or climb, find someone's zodiac animal from 我屬猴, count the animals in a pen, build sentences.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 羊兔虎.

## Words
Groups: `animals` (隻 for most, 條 for long ones), `words`, `verbs`, `signs`, `pets`, `measures`, `ones`, `borrowed`, and the derived `counts`, `which`, `zodiac` (in order, each with `animal`), `asks`, `answers`, `whichCan`, `can`, `cant` (with `animal`, `verb`, `can`), `sentences`.
`V.abilities` lists, for each verb, the animals that clearly can and clearly can't: only those are ever asked about, so add an animal there only when the answer is obvious.
條 is `tiu` (unit 5) and 跳 `tiu3` (unit 16). 走 is unit 8's `zau` ("without"), borrowed as "to run".

## Audio
