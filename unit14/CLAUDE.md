# Unit 14 · Body & Health 身體

Body parts, 痛, 睇醫生, 食藥. Grammar point: 咗 (done), 有冇 questions.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u14-learn`).
- `doctor.html`, `doctor.js` (`u14-doctor`): Doctor's Visit: hear body parts, aches and symptoms, say whose with a measure word (`Measures.round`), answer the doctor's 有冇 and 咗未 questions from a patient's chart, follow a prescription, build sentences.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 口耳身.

## Words
Groups: `body` (隻 for one of a pair and teeth, 個 for the rest, 條 for the throat), `health`, `symptoms`, `grammar`, `counting`, `measures`, `borrowed`, and the derived `aches` (頭痛, with `part`), `mine`, `todo` (with `pic`), `phrases`, `asks` (with `about` and `answers`), `done`, `rx` (prescriptions, with `times` and `pills`), `sentences`.

## Audio
`Units.phonemes` reads 好返 (`hou-faan`) from jyutping: 返 is faan2 here, but faan1 in writing.

## Drawings
A body part is ringed in gold on a face (close up) or a whole figure (`face`, `figure`); an ache is the same picture with the ring in red, "ouch" lines and a pained face. Symptoms, the doctor and prescriptions (a box of pills for each time a day) use the same parts (`inBed`, `zzz`, `drop`, `puff`).

## Used by later units
The body parts (`hand`, `head`, `eye`, `stomach`... with their pictures) and aches (`head-tung3`...), 醫生, 藥, 休息, 咗 (`zo2`), 冇 (`mou`), 未, 次, 日, 粒 (`nap`, unit 20's fruit).
