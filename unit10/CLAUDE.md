# Unit 10 · Family 屋企人

爸爸 媽媽 哥哥 家姐 細佬 細妹, 爺爺 嫲嫲 公公 婆婆, 老公 / 老婆, 仔 / 女. Grammar point: possessive 嘅 and measure (我嘅書, 我媽媽, 我個仔).

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u10-learn`).
- `tree.html`, `tree.js` (`u10-tree`): Family Tree: find people on the tree, name them, work out 嘅 chains (爸爸嘅媽媽), say whose things are whose (`Measures.round`), build sentences.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 女仔公.

## Words
Groups: `parents`, `siblings`, `grandparents`, `own`, `words`, `measures`, `things`, `borrowed`, and the derived `relations` (嘅 chains, each with `means`: the member's id), `whose`, `mine`, `sentences`. `member: true` marks one person on the tree, for Family Tree and the listen quiz.

## Audio
女 on its own (`daughter`, neoi2) uses MiniMax: Azure says `neoi 2` alone like neoi5.

## Drawings
Each family word is a small family tree: 我 in gold, the person or people meant in colour with an arrow, the rest grey. Tree A is the family you grow up in (grandparents, parents, brothers and sisters); tree B is your own (partner and children).

The family words (`dad`, `elder-sister`...) with their family-tree pictures, 嘅, 同, 住 (`zyu`), and 屋企 (`home`; units 11, 15, 21).
