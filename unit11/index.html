Unit 11

出街Getting Around

港鐵, 巴士 and 的士, asking 喺邊度呀？, and finding the way: 直行, 轉左, 喺右邊.

Learn

Places and directions

Transport and places around town, 去 and 喺, then left, right, straight on and across the road.

 Game

Route

Follow directions on a street map, and tell people the way: 轉左，喺右邊.

 Game

Tone Detective

Hear a place or a direction and pick its tones. 喺 or 係?

 Speaking

Say It Back

Record yourself and compare your tones with a native speaker's pitch curve.
