# Unit 11 · Getting Around 出街

港鐵, 巴士, 的士, 喺邊度, 左 / 右, 直行. Grammar point: 喺 (be at), 去 + place.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u11-learn`).
- `route.html`, `route.js` (`u11-route`): Route: hear places and transport, follow directions on a crossroads map (直行 / 轉左 / 轉右, then 喺左邊 / 喺右邊), say where a place is, build sentences with 喺 and 去.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 左右去.

## Words
Groups: `verbs`, `where`, `transport`, `places`, `way`, `borrowed`, `elsewhere`, and the derived `stations`, `phrases`, `directions` (with `turn`: zik / zo / jau, and `side`: zo / jau, which Route places on its map), `questions` (with `place`), `sentences`.

## Audio
`Units.phonemes` reads 巴士 (`bus`), 的士 (`taxi`) and 近 (`kan`) from jyutping in every phrase; 轉 (`zyun`) and two 喺 phrases (`hai2-zo-bin`, `bank-hai2-deoi-min`) have `phoneme: true` of their own. 喺 is not in the `Units.phonemes` list: reading it from jyutping everywhere upset the words around it.

## Drawings
Transport and the places Route puts on its map (`car`, `plane` from `svg.mjs`, the rest local).

## Used by later units
The places and transport with their pictures, 去 (`heoi`), 喺, 搭, 邊度, 度 (`dou6`), 出街 (`ceot-street`), 出 (`ceot`), 嚟 (`lai`).
