# Unit 5 · Measure Words 量詞

個 隻 本 張 條 枝 架 件 杯 碗 對 啲, nouns sorted by shape. Grammar point: number + measure + noun (三隻貓); measure + noun = "the" (隻貓); 呢 / 嗰.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u5-learn`).
- `sort.html`, `sort.js` (`u5-sort`): Measure Sort: pick a thing's measure word, sort things by measure, hear them, point with 呢 or 嗰.
- `count.html`, `count.js` (`u5-count`): Count It: count pictures and say how many, hear a count and find it, build sentences with 有 and 幾多.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 本杯有.

## Words
Groups: `measures`, `things`, `words`, `borrowed`, and the derived `ones` (一隻貓), `the` (隻貓), `counts`, `thisThat` (呢隻貓), `sentences`. A thing's `measure` is a measure word's id; a measure with a `dish` (杯 cup, 碗 bowl) must match the picture (check.mjs). `counted: [one, many]` where "a <english>" / "<english>s" is wrong.
Borrows 個 (`go`) from unit 4, and unit 1's 魚 車 水 雞 牛, giving each a `measure` (一條魚, 一杯水).

## Audio
`phoneme: true` on 枱 (`table`, and every phrase with it), 呢 (`ni`) and 呀 (`aa`).

## Drawings
One per noun, grouped by measure word: flat, outlined, with a soft shadow. Unit 19's animals and unit 20's fruit follow this style.

## Used by later units
The measure words (`zek` 隻, `tiu` 條, `gin` 件, `wun` 碗, `di` 啲...), 呢 (`ni`), 有 (`jau`), and the things with their pictures and `one-<id>` phrases. Unit 18 takes them by way of unit 6, with prices.
