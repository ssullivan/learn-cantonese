# Unit 18 · Comparing 比較

大 / 細, 快 / 慢, 高 / 矮, 平 / 貴. Grammar point: A 比 B + adjective, and 貴過, 好多 / 少少, 冇…咁, 一樣, 最.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u18-learn`).
- `compare.html`, `compare.js` (`u18-compare`): Which Is Bigger: hear 邊個平啲呀？ and tap the one, find the biggest or cheapest with 最, say whether a comparison is right (啱) or wrong, choose 好多 or 少少, work out which one 冇…咁 means, say how much older a brother or sister is, build sentences. Every price, size, speed, age and height it uses comes from vocab.js.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 比高快.

## Words
Things and people carry what can be compared: `price` (dollars, from unit 6), `size` (cm), `speed` (km/h), `age` (years), `height` (cm). `V.scales` names each scale's adjectives, more (大, 貴...) and less (細, 平...). The derived sentences carry `cmp { a, b, scale, adj, holds }`: a is adj-er than b (on 冇…咁, b isn't as adj as a), and `holds` (worked out in vocab.js) says whether the data agrees.
Groups: `adjectives`, `talk`, `things`, `people`, `measures`, `borrowed`, `words`, `ages` (`y1`…`y9`), and the derived `bei`, `gwo`, `howMuch`, `howOld`, `notAs`, `same`, `which`, `most`, `ding`, `best`, `sentences`. Takes unit 6's things with their prices (pictures stay in unit 5) and unit 10's family members for ages.

## Audio
`Units.phonemes` reads 平 (`peng`), 雞蛋 (`egg`), 小巴 (`minibus`) and 魚 (`fish`) from jyutping in every phrase; borrowed words like 枱 巴士 的士 keep the `phoneme` they have at home.

## Drawings
Each adjective as a contrast with a gold arrow on the one meant (大 細 高 矮), a fast car and a slow snail, a podium for 最, two balls the same for 一樣 (`ball`, `figure`, `arrow`, `car`).

## Borrowed by later units
The adjectives 大 細 高 矮 快 慢 (`daai`, `sai`, `gou`, `ai`, `faai`, `maan`, with their pictures), 比, 最 (`zeoi`, unit 20), 一樣, 差唔多, 啱 / 唔啱, 好多, the ages. A later unit's things can be compared by adding a `size`, `speed`... (unit 19's animals could add a `size`).
