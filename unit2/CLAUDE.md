# Unit 2 · Greetings 打招呼

你好, 早晨 / 早唞, 拜拜, 最近點呀, 唔該 vs 多謝 and their replies, 對唔住 vs 唔好意思. Grammar point: politeness, 唔該 (for a service) vs 多謝 (for a gift or a kindness).

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u2-learn`).
- `reply.html` (`u2-reply`): Reply Match (`shared/reply.js`): hear a phrase, answer back, or pick what to say in a situation.
- `tones.html`, `say.html` (`u2-tones`, `u2-say`).
- `sheet.html`, `write.html` (`u2-write`): writes 你早多 and the Cantonese 冇咗哋. 冇 咗 哋 have no Hong Kong standard, so they are composed from checked parts in `tools/strokes-composed.mjs` (咗: the 口 of 呢, then 左).

## Words
Groups: `greetings`, `howAreYou`, `polite`. Reply Match reads `reply` (ids of good answers) and `when` (situations; the picture shows the first).

## Drawings
`art.mjs` draws the situation each phrase is for (`when`), not the phrase: a sunrise for 早晨, a teapot and cup (`teapot`, `teacup`) for thanks.
