Unit 6

買嘢Money & Shopping

幾多錢呀？ 三蚊半, 百五蚊, 好平, 好貴: prices, paying, and cheap or expensive.

Learn

Money and prices

蚊 and 毫, 三蚊半 and 百五蚊, 幾多錢, 好 + adjective, and 要 and 買.

 Game

Market Stall

Hear prices, pay the right amount in coins and notes, and say 好平 or 好貴.

 Game

Tone Detective

Hear a price or a shopping word and pick its tones. 買 or 賣?

 Speaking

Say It Back

Record yourself and compare your tones with a native speaker's pitch curve.
