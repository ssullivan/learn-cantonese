# Unit 15 · Daily Routine 日常

起身, 返工, 食飯, 瞓覺. Grammar point: 緊 (-ing), 過 (ever), with 咗 and 未 from unit 14.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u15-learn`).
- `planner.html`, `planner.js` (`u15-planner`): Day Planner: hear what someone does and when, put two things in order, read a day planner to say what they're doing now (緊), what's done (咗) or not yet (未), answer 過 questions from a chart, build sentences. The planner builds unit 9 clock ids in its script (`Units.word(9, 't0700')`).
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 先做起.

## Words
Activities are a verb and a thing (返 + 工), so 緊, 咗 and 過 go between them (返緊工). Groups: `verbs`, `things`, `grammar`, `order`, `measures`, `borrowed`, and the derived `activities` (with `at`: [hour, minute] on a 24-hour clock, `ing`, `done`), `forms` (with `act` and `form`: zo / gan / mei / ask), `asks`, `times` (from unit 9, made by `Canto.time`), `when`, `sequence` (with `first` and `then`), `ever`, `everAsks` (with `item`, `answers`, `pic`), `sentences`. 食 飲 睇 著 瞓 去 搭 are borrowed.

## Audio
`Units.phonemes` reads 返 (`faan`), 著 (`zoek`) and 喇 (`laa3`) from jyutping in every phrase.

## Drawings
One per activity. Going to work or school is someone walking to the building's door, finishing is walking away from it; a sun or moon in the top-left corner says when (morning, midday, evening, night).

## Borrowed by later units
The activities (`hei-san`, `faan-gung`... with their pictures and their 咗 / 緊 / 未 forms), 返, 做, 緊 (`gan`), 過 (`gwo`), 通常, 先 / 然後, 之前 / 之後, and 放 / 洗 / 煮 (`fong`, `sai`, `zyu`, unit 21).
