Unit 17

心情Feelings

開心, 攰, 嬲, 驚, 悶: how you feel (我好攰), asking 你開唔開心呀？, and the particles that colour a sentence: 啦 喇 喎 囉 嘛.

Learn

How do you feel?

Happy, sad, tired, hungry; 好 / 有啲 + feeling, 開唔開心, 點解 and 因為, and five sentence particles.

 Game

Particle Match

Find the face, cheer up a friend with 啦, and match each situation to its particle: 啦, 喇, 喎, 囉 or 嘛?

 Game

Tone Detective

Hear feelings and particles and pick their tones. 驚 or 頸? 啦 or 喇?

 Speaking

Say It Back

Record yourself and compare your tones with a native speaker's pitch curve.
