# Unit 17 · Feelings 心情

開心, 攰, 嬲, 驚, 悶. Grammar point: sentence particles 啦 喇 喎 囉 嘛.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u17-learn`).
- `particle.html`, `particle.js` (`u17-particles`): Particle Match: hear a feeling and find its face, answer 開唔開心 about a face, comfort a friend with 啦, pick the particle a situation calls for, hear what a particle sentence means, build sentences.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 因考法.

## Words
Groups: `feelings`, `halves` (first syllables, for A唔A: 開唔開心; a two-syllable feeling's `half` names its own), `talk`, `particles`, `measures`, `borrowed`, and the derived `nots` (唔開心), `states` (我好攰), `asks` (with `feeling`, `answers`), `comfort` (with `for`), `particled` (with `particle`, `why`: the situation in English, `also`: other particles that could fit), `sentences`. 啦 / 喇 are unit 13's.

## Audio
`Units.phonemes` reads every particle (`laa1`, `laa3`, `wo3`, `lo1`, `maa3`) and 好返 (`hou-faan`, as in unit 14) from jyutping in every phrase.

## Drawings
A face for each feeling and for 唔開心, from `face()`'s expressions (mouth, eyes, brows), with local touches (rosy cheeks, sparkles, a tear).

## Borrowed by later units
The feelings (`hoi-sam`, `gui`, `tou-ngo`... with their faces, 唔開心 as `m-hoi-sam`, and each `half`), 我好X as `ngo-hou-<id>`, 心情, 覺得, 點解, 因為, 咁 (`gam3`, unit 18), 考試, 開 (`hoi`, unit 21), and the particles 喎 囉 嘛 (`wo3`, `lo1`, `maa3`).
