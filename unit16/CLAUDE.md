# Unit 16 · Likes & Hobbies 興趣

鍾意, 睇戲, 打波, 唱K. Grammar point: 鍾意 + verb (鍾唔鍾意), 想, 識 / 會.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u16-learn`).
- `survey.html`, `survey.js` (`u16-survey`): Survey: hear a hobby, answer 鍾唔鍾意 and 識唔識 from your survey sheet, note what someone says they like, ask the right question (鍾唔鍾意, 識唔識 or 想唔想), build sentences.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 山相想.

## Words
Hobbies are a verb and a thing (游 + 水), like unit 15's day, so 過 and 緊 still go between them (游過水). Groups: `verbs`, `things`, `grammar`, `talk`, `measures`, `borrowed`, and the derived `hobbies` (with `ing`, `out`: you go out for it, so 想去 + it, and `skill`: you learn it, so 識 + it), `likes`, `cans` (with `hobby` and `yes`), `answers`, `asks` (with `hobby`, `kind`: like / can / want, `answers`), `sentences`. 打 睇 做 are borrowed.

## Audio
