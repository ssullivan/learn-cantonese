# Cantonese Learning Games

Static HTML Cantonese lessons and games served with GitHub Pages at https://ssullivan.github.io/learn-cantonese/ (from `main`, repo root). No build step for the site itself; `tools/` scripts generate assets and check the site.

Maintainability and DRY come first: anything two pages or units share belongs in `shared/` (browser) or `tools/` (Node), not in copies. Generated files are never edited by hand; edit their source and regenerate.

Each `unit<N>/CLAUDE.md` holds that unit's specifics: its topic and grammar point, games, audio and drawing quirks, and what later units borrow from it. Read it before working in a unit, and when borrowing from one. Keep it up to date when the unit changes.

## Words, audio and pictures
- Every word lives once, in `unit<N>/vocab.js`: `{ id, hanzi, jyutping, english, note?, img?, measure?, say?, ssml?, phoneme?, voice?, reply?, when?, words? }` plus the unit's own fields (documented in its header). Every field is listed with the kind of value it holds in `tools/vocab-fields.mjs`, and check.mjs fails on any other field, a wrong value, or an id that names no entry: add a new field there first. `id` names its files; `img: false` means no picture. `reply` (ids of good answers) and `when` (situations) feed Reply Match; `words` (the ids a derived sentence is made of) feeds Tiles.round.
- The dictionary, `words/words.js`, holds words once with the unit that teaches them (`taught`); their audio and picture are in `words/`. A unit takes them with `Words.get(id)` or `Words.list('a b c')`, adding its own fields (`{ ...Words.get('milk-tea'), drink: ... }`), and keeps its phrases and sentences, whose audio stays in the unit. check.mjs fails when a unit uses a word taught after it, or the teaching unit doesn't list it. Units are moving their words there; until a unit has, its words are in its `vocab.js` as below.
- A word belongs to the first unit that teaches it. A later unit borrows it with `Units.word(n, id)` in the derived section of its `vocab.js`; its audio and picture stay in `unit<n>/`. Borrowing a borrowed word keeps its home unit and what was added to it. Borrow only from earlier units; never copy a word into a second vocab.js. A word's id is unique on the whole site (when two words would share one, one gets its tone number: 大 `daai6`, 戴 `daai`), and so is its hanzi, jyutping and voice; check.mjs enforces both. A later unit that uses a word in another sense borrows it and overrides `english` and `note` (unit 13's 焗 is unit 7's `baked`). Phrases need only be unique in their unit.
- Derive, don't copy: phrases and sentences are computed at the bottom of `vocab.js` (`Units.sentences`), and get audio like any entry. Numbers, counts and times come from `Canto.number` and `Canto.time`, prices from `Canto.price`, never typed out. Extend `shared/numbers.js` (and `tools/numbers.test.mjs`) when a unit needs a new form.
- Measure words: a thing's `measure` must match its picture (籠 ↔ `steamer()`, 碟 ↔ `plate()`, 碗 ↔ `bowl()`, 杯 ↔ `cup()`, no dish for the rest); check.mjs enforces it, in a borrowed word's own unit. A borrowed noun can gain a `measure`.
- Jyutping has a tone number on every syllable (`haa1 gaau2`). Double-check changed tones (腸粉 coeng2, 燒賣 maai2) and words with several readings.
- Colloquial Cantonese (佢, 係, 唔, 咗), not Mandarin or written forms.
- Pictures: add a drawing to `art.mjs`, run `node tools/draw.mjs` (which also deletes svgs no longer drawn). Reuse or extend `tools/svg.mjs` parts instead of copying markup. 128×128 flat style, fixed colors, transparent background, a `<title>`. Render and look at new drawings before committing.

### Audio
- `node tools/tts.mjs [words|unitN ...] [--only id,id] [--force]` regenerates only entries whose text or voice changed, saving `manifest.json` after every clip. Without `--only` it also moves a renamed entry's clip (and audio-check verdict) to its new id, or from a unit to `words/` when the word moves to the dictionary (both in the same run), and deletes clips of entries that are gone, so renaming or moving costs no new audio. Credentials come from `~/.config/learning-cantonese/config.env` (`AZURE_SPEECH_KEY`, `AZURE_SPEECH_REGION`, `MINIMAX_KEY`), read by `tools/site.mjs`. **Never put a key in any committed file** (code, docs, fixtures, logs, commit messages) and never print one; check.mjs fails if any file holds one.
- A misread word: `phoneme: true` reads its jyutping exactly (particles 呀 嗎 呢, changed tones like 名 meng2), or an `ssml` override (zh-HK accepts only the `sapi` alphabet: `<phoneme alphabet="sapi" ph="je 6 maan 1">`). On a phrase, `phoneme: [ids]` reads only those words from jyutping; `Units.phonemes(V, ids)` sets it on every entry using them. Reading one word that way can upset its neighbours, so keep the list to words the check shows it helps.
- If Azure can't say a syllable at all (it has no nin2, no ning2, no neoi2 alone), give that word `voice: 'minimax:Cantonese_ProfessionalHost（F)'`. tts.mjs makes up to five MiniMax takes and keeps the first that passes audio-check. Keep it to the few words that need it: MiniMax failed single-syllable tone drills that Azure passed, and it sounds unlike the rest of the site.
- Azure sometimes answers 401 to a burst; tts.mjs retries twice (`shouldRetryAzure`), so a wrong key still fails fast.

### Verifying audio
Run after every `tts.mjs` run. The checker is a separate repo, **audio-lang-tools** (`~/audio-lang-tools` or `$AUDIO_LANG_TOOLS`; see its CLAUDE.md); `tools/audio-check.mjs` is the adapter. AUDIO-CHECKING.md explains it for people.
1. `node tools/audio-check.mjs unit<N>` or `words` (`--only id,id`, `--all`, `--json out.json`; cached by clip). It decodes each clip (broken, silent, clipped, wrong length), transcribes it with Azure speech-to-text and compares sounds ignoring tone, and scores each syllable's tone by forced alignment and a model trained on that voice (HiuMaan, WanLung, HiuGaai; MiniMax only on shape, so only LISTEN). `媽4→1 (0.12)` means another tone is likelier.
2. **CHECK**: a file problem or a clearly unlikely tone. **LISTEN**: other sounds heard, or a doubtful tone.
3. For each CHECK, find out whether the voice or the measurement is wrong: swap an SSML variant into the clip (plain, `phoneme: true`, explicit sapi) and rerun `--only <id>`; a variant that matches a reading exactly is what the voice says. Restore with `git checkout unit<N>/audio/`.
4. Fix it in `vocab.js`, run `tts.mjs --only <id>`, rerun the check.
5. Commit `audio/check.json`. Tell the user which clips are still flagged, so a listener starts there.

Limits: the check asks whether each tone is the voice's own version, not whether two tones sound apart (teaching clips that contrast tones need a listener; see unit 1). About 8% of right clips get a CHECK and 20% some flag, many real (question-final 呀 said high, tone 5 like 2, 魚 jyu2). Speech-to-text is weak on bare syllables and biased to common words. The checker's accuracy is measured in audio-lang-tools (`uv run altools bench score`, with recorded floors), not assumed; change the checker there. `node tools/audio-check.mjs --words out.json` exports this site's words for `altools bench make`.

Human listeners use `review/` (linked from the home page); a clip regenerated after it was marked says so. Its vocab scripts are written by `check.mjs --fix`.

## Writing characters
Hong Kong's standard rules: stroke order and form as in the Education Bureau's 《香港小學學習字詞表》 (EDB). The free data, Make Me a Hanzi (`hanzi-writer-data`), follows mainland order: about one character in seven differs, and some forms differ (之 and 艹 have 4 strokes in Hong Kong). STROKE-ORDER.md explains it for people.
- Each unit's vocab has `write: '...'`: three characters from its own words (check.mjs enforces each is in one of its words and taught by one unit only).
- `node tools/stroke-check.mjs [unit<N> | 之菜]` plays the EDB animations in headless Chrome, matches each stroke's ink to Make Me a Hanzi's, and writes `tools/strokes-hk.json`: **ok**; **look** (compare on `review/strokes.html`, then `--confirm <chars>`); **differs** (other stroke count: can't be taught from this data); **missing** (not in EDB, or no data). Keep only these facts from EDB, never its drawings. Needs Playwright outside the repo: `npm install --prefix ~/.local/share/learning-cantonese playwright` (or `$PLAYWRIGHT_DIR`).
- Cantonese characters (咗 哋 冇 喺 啲 嗰...) have no standard to check. `tools/strokes-composed.mjs` builds them from checked parts, left part first, matching a dictionary's stroke count; review marks them "Composed". Look at a new one rendered before teaching it. Not composable yet: 佢 (Hong Kong's 巨 has 5 strokes, the data 4).
- Then `node tools/strokes.mjs` writes `strokes/<hex>.json` (each says how it was changed). check.mjs fails until every taught character has an ok or confirmed record and an up to date file.
- Each unit has `sheet.html` and `write.html` (copy unit 4's) with cards on its page; Write It's key is `u<N>-write`. Print-test a sheet in A4 and Letter (Playwright `page.pdf({ preferCSSPageSize: true })`): each character's block stays on one page.

## Pages
- Load `shared/` with `../shared/...`; relative links only (the site is served under `/learn-cantonese/`).
- Every page links the favicon right after its viewport meta: `<link rel="icon" href="../favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">` (`favicon.svg` on the root page).
- Local CSS and JS carry a `?v=<hash>` stamp written by `node tools/check.mjs --fix`; never edit one by hand.
- Templates: `unit7/learn.html` + `lessons.js` for learn pages, `unit7/trolley.*` for games. Reusable UI goes in `shared/`, not a unit's script.
- Script order: `core.js`, `audio.js`, then `learn.js` or `game.js` (+ `tones.js`, `reply.js`...), then the markers `<!-- vocab: written by node tools/check.mjs --fix -->` … `<!-- /vocab -->`, then the page script. `--fix` writes the vocab scripts between the markers (every earlier unit's vocab too, since page scripts borrow); never write them by hand.
- Show Chinese with Jyutping through `Canto.zh(hanzi, jyutping)`, never hand-built spans; mixed English and Chinese text goes through `Canto.tagZh` (gives the Chinese `lang="zh-HK"`). Ruby over each character on words on their own at 700px+, Jyutping after the word in prose and on phones; a word whose characters and syllables don't pair up (卅 saa1 aa6) keeps it after.
- No emoji in the UI; use text, ▶, ★, or a Chinese character badge (`Canto.speech`).
- Game and learn pages link back with `href="./"` ("← Unit N"); unit and review pages with `href="../"` ("← All units").
- A new learn page or game gets a card on `unit<N>/index.html`.
- The root `index.html` lists every unit by stage (`h2.stage-head`); units not built are `div.card.soon` placeholders. A new unit: create `unit<N>/` like `unit7/` (with its CLAUDE.md), turn its placeholder into an `a.card` with a picture, and keep the "N units" eyebrow right.
- `localStorage` is shared site-wide: keys `u<N>-learn`, `u<N>-<game>`.
- `.nojekyll` serves files as-is. The site is all rights reserved (`LICENSE`); the copyright line comes from `.page::after` in `theme.css`, so no footers.
- Avoid gendered pronouns for named people in lessons; reword instead.

## Checking a change
1. `node tools/check.mjs --fix` must print "All checks passed". Tests must be able to fail: after writing one, break the code on purpose and confirm it's reported. The same checks run before every commit and on every push; never skip the hook with `--no-verify`.
2. Serve locally (`python3 -m http.server`) and try the page at 390px and desktop, light and dark: no horizontal scroll, all audio plays, quizzes and levels complete, timers, stars and unlocks work.
