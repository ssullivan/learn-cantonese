Learn

Colours and clothes

 Game

Write It

Write 白, 色 and 紅 stroke by stroke with your finger: trace, then from memory, then 默書 from what you hear.

 Writing

Print a practice sheet: each character stroke by stroke in Hong Kong order, to trace and then write.

 Game
