# Unit 21 · Kitchen Appliances 廚房電器

廚房, 雪櫃 微波爐 焗爐 煤氣爐 電飯煲 水煲 多士爐 洗碗機, 部 for machines; 叮 煲 炒 整, 放入 / 攞出, 開 / 閂, 壞咗. Grammar point: 用 + appliance + verb (用水煲煲水); how long goes after the verb (叮兩分鐘, 幾耐), when before it.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u21-learn`).
- `kitchen.html`, `kitchen.js` (`u21-kitchen`): Kitchen Helper 幫手: hear an appliance and find it, hear 用乜嘢煲飯呀？ and pick the appliance for the job, say what an appliance is for, hear how long to cook something (叮兩分鐘), build sentences.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 用入電.

## Words
Tasks are a verb and a thing (煲 + 水), like unit 16's hobbies. Groups: `appliances` (each with `task`), `words`, `verbs`, `measures` (部), `food`, `borrowed`, and the derived `tasks` (with `tool`: the appliance), `uses`, `asks`, `minutes` (`mins<n>`: `Canto.number` with 分鐘), `howLong` (with `mins`), `inOut`, `sentences`, `ones`. The last unit, so it borrows from nearly every earlier one (unit 15's 放 洗 煮, unit 13's 雪 for 雪櫃, unit 9's `t0600`).

## Audio
`phoneme: true` on 煤氣爐 (`stove`), 多士 (`toast`, as in unit 8) and 呀.

## Drawings
