# Unit 13 · Weather 天氣

熱, 凍, 落雨, 打風, 季節, temperatures. Grammar point: 好 / 幾 / 太 + adjective, and 啦 (advice).

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u13-learn`).
- `forecast.html`, `forecast.js` (`u13-forecast`): Forecast: hear the weather and the temperature, tell 好熱 from 太熱, give advice with 啦, find the day in a forecast, build sentences.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 大天雨.

## Words
Temperatures (`c<n>`, from `Canto.number`), degree phrases, advice and sentences are derived at the bottom of vocab.js. Groups: `basics`, `weather`, `feel`, `seasons`, `grammar`, `things`, `days`, `weekdays`, `measures`, `borrowed`, `temps`, `phrases`, `degrees` (with `adj` and `degree`), `advice` (with `for`: the weathers it suits, the first is its picture in Forecast), `sentences`. Borrows 凍 / 熱 from unit 8 and weekdays from unit 9.

## Audio
`phoneme: true` on 啦 and 喇 (`laa1`, `laa3`) and every phrase ending in one, and on `c31`.

## Drawings
Weather icons built from a few local parts (sun, cloud, rain, snowflake, lightning), a thermometer for 好熱 and 好凍, the four seasons, and an umbrella.

## Borrowed by later units
The weather (`lok-jyu`, `tin-cing`..., and 好熱 / 好凍 as `hou-jit`, `hou-dung`, with their pictures), the seasons, temperatures (`c25`), 太, 會 (`wui`, unit 19), 啦 / 喇 (`laa1`, `laa3`, unit 17), 帶 / 遮, 雪 (`syut`, unit 21's 雪櫃).
