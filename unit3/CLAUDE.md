# Unit 3 · Me & You 我同你

我 你 佢 (+ 哋), 我叫…, 係 / 唔係, 邊個, 乜嘢. Grammar point: 係, 唔 negation, A唔A questions (係唔係), and 呀 / 嗎.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u3-learn`): separate listen quizzes for pronouns and for people words.
- `builder.html`, `builder.js` (`u3-builder`): Question Builder: build sentences and questions from word tiles (`Tiles.round`), and answer 係呀 / 唔係呀 about a picture.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`; `sheet.html`, `write.html`: writes 人我名.

## Words
Groups: `people`, `words`, `things`, `asking`, and the derived `statements`, `maa`, `aNotA`, `wh`, `answers`. Sentences keep their word ids in `words`, which Question Builder turns into tiles.

## Audio
`phoneme: true` on the particles 呀 嗎 呢 (`aa`, `maa`, `ne`), on 名 (`meng`, meng2) and 英文 (`jing-man`): the voice misreads them from characters.

## Drawings
The pronouns share one scene: the speaker (left, red, speech bubble) talks to the listener (right) with a third person at the back. The speaker points at the person or people meant, coloured (listener blue, others green) inside a gold ring; the rest are grey. Person words (man, woman...) use other colours so they never look like a pronoun.

## Used by later units
Almost every unit borrows its pronouns and 係 / 唔係 / 乜嘢 / 邊個 (`ngo`, `nei`, `keoi`, `ngo-dei`, `bin-go`...), along with `aa` (呀) and its `phoneme`.
