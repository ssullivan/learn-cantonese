# Unit 4 · Numbers 數字

零–十, 百 千 萬, 廿 / 卅, 零 in the gaps, 幾多, 第. Grammar point: 二 vs 兩.

## Pages
- `learn.html` (`u4-learn`): the listen quiz answers are digits.
- `dash.html`, `dash.js` (`u4-dash`): Number Dash: hear a number and tap it (十四 or 四十?), say numbers, pick 二 or 兩.
- `tones.html`, `say.html`.
- `sheet.html`, `write.html` (`u4-write`): writes 一二三十. **The template** for every unit's sheet and Write It pages.

## Words
All numbers come from `Canto.number` (`shared/numbers.js`) at the bottom of vocab.js, never typed out; each has `n` (its value). Groups: `words`, `numbers` (0–99: `n0`…`n99`), `big`, `ordinals`, `twos`, `short` (short forms like 卅一). 個 (`go`) is taught here.

## Audio
`phoneme: true` on the short forms (`short-31`, `short-45`...): read from characters they come out as the long form.

## Drawings
0–10 are counters on a ten frame, two rows of five (6 is a full row and one more), not hand signs.

## Borrowed by later units
個 (`go`, by nearly every unit), 兩 (`loeng`), 幾多 (`gei-do`), and the numbers `n1`…`n99` (unit 7's portions, unit 11's `n10`). Later units make new numbers with `Canto.number` rather than borrowing them.
